Principles

Chamfer, never roundOuter corners cut at 2u, small parts at 1.5u. Diagonals run at 45° wherever the form allows.

One weight1.75 stroke at every size, square caps, mitred joins. No fills, no gradients, no glow.

Solid notchesA filled rivet or notch only where it carries meaning: a pin-1 marker, an alert dot, a date.

Colour by contextInk #0E0D12 at rest, purple #5B2BE0 when active, white on solid buttons.
